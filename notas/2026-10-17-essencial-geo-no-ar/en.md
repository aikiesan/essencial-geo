Essencial Geo starts today as three things at once: a **feed** of thematic maps of Brazil, an
**open-data repository** and a **cartography portfolio** that grows in public. Each plate comes
with the files that produced it (GeoPackage, DXF, CSV), with its projection and sources declared
and — from the maps with real data onwards — with its own DOI on Zenodo, so that it can be cited
in a paper, a design drawing or an academic CV.

## What is here already

Plates 001 to 004 are **sample plates**: they use synthetic data generated by a script, and exist
to set the visual language of the series — gridded density, relief, land use and figure–ground.
They carry a “synthetic data” label and do not receive a DOI.

## Two languages, always

Every map, technical record and note is published in **Brazilian Portuguese** and **British
English**. The PT · EN switch at the top of the left-hand column takes you to the same page in the
other language. The plates themselves are exported in both versions.

## Dates, as in a newspaper

Every item carries its date in full and, where relevant, the date of its last update. Items are
scheduled: they go live at 09:00 Brasília time (12:00 UTC) on their publication day.

## How to follow

Subscribe to the RSS feed (there is one per language) or watch the repository on GitHub.
Corrections and suggestions for new extents are welcome as *issues*.
