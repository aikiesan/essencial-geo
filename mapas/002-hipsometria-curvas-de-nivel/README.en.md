# Hypsometry and contour lines

<p class="so-github"><a href="README.md">Português</a> · <strong>English</strong></p>

> **Sample plate.** The relief is synthetic and does not represent any real place.

## Method

1. An elevation field is generated by fractal noise (6 octaves) added to a *ridged*
   crest concentrated along a winding axis.
2. Values are rescaled to 620–2,380 m.
3. Isolines are extracted every 50 m with *marching squares* (`d3-contour`).
4. The bands receive earthy hypsometric tints — forest green in the valleys, ochre
   and clay on the slopes, greyish pink on the summits.

## Conventions of the relief series

- **Index** contour every 250 m (1.5 px stroke), **intermediate** every 50 m (0.7 px).
- The DXF splits the contours into the layers `CURVAS_MESTRAS_250M` and
  `CURVAS_INTERMEDIARIAS_50M`, with the height stored as each polyline’s elevation —
  in AutoCAD or Rhino, the contours arrive at the right height.

## Version with real data

```bash
# 5 m contours from a clipped and reprojected DEM
gdalwarp -t_srs EPSG:31983 -r bilinear mde.tif mde_utm.tif
gdal_contour -a cota -i 5 mde_utm.tif curvas.gpkg
ogr2ogr -f DXF curvas.dxf curvas.gpkg
```
